Thrym is a strength training log that works completely offline. Plan your training, log your sets quickly at the gym and follow your progress over time. Your data stays on your device: no account, no ads, no tracking, and the app does not request internet access.

<b>Workouts</b>
• Start a workout from a plan, from a meta-plan or as a free session
• Sets are prefilled with weight and reps, so you only confirm or adjust them
• Optional details per set: RPE or RIR, set type (warm-up, drop, failure), set intention and a plate calculator
• Rest timer, supersets and adding several exercises at once
• New personal records are detected while you train
• Finish early with open sets and get a summary of what you actually did

<b>Plans and meta-plans</b>
• Plans with target sets, reps and weight, or individual targets for each set
• Meta-plans rotate through several plans (for example Push, Pull, Legs) and suggest the next one; you can skip a suggestion
• A progression scheme per exercise in a plan: linear, double progression, total reps or RPE/RIR

<b>Progression coach</b>
• After a workout the coach suggests new targets and shows the reason for each suggestion
• Your plan only changes after you confirm: accept, edit or discard each suggestion
• A missed target is repeated; repeated misses lead to a back-off suggestion

<b>Recovery and volume</b>
• Optional daily check-in for sleep, energy, stress and muscle soreness
• Training readiness and deload hints based on your recent workouts
• Deload mode with half the sets or 15 % less weight
• Weekly volume per muscle group with MEV/MAV ranges and an 8-week trend

<b>History and statistics</b>
• All workouts with search and filters by time period and plan
• Correct or delete single sets afterwards and add a workout note
• Exercise library with search, muscle filter, custom exercises and archiving
• Per exercise: personal records, estimated one-rep max (e1RM) and a progress chart

<b>Settings and data</b>
• kg or lb, first day of the week, two visual styles, seven color schemes, light, dark or system theme and dynamic color
• Plates and bar weight for the plate calculator, rest timer and workout reminders
• Export and import backups as a local file, with an optional backup reminder; an internal safety backup is created before every import

Thrym is available in English and German. A native iOS version is built from the same source code. The source code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

Thrym was previously called IronLog. To move your data, export a backup in IronLog and import it in Thrym.
